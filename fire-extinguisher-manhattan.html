Fire Extinguisher Service for Manhattan Businesses

Manhattan contains a wide variety of commercial properties, including restaurants, office buildings, retail stores, hotels and mixed-use commercial properties. Fire extinguisher service needs can vary depending on the type of business, the property and the equipment in use.

Manhattan Restaurants & Commercial Kitchens

Restaurants and food-service businesses should keep portable fire extinguishers visible and accessible. Commercial kitchens may also use separate fire suppression equipment designed for cooking-related fire hazards.

Midtown Offices & Commercial Buildings

Office buildings and commercial properties may contain multiple floors, tenants and fire extinguishers. Property managers and commercial customers with multiple units can contact us to discuss their service needs.

Retail Stores & Street-Level Businesses

Retail stores in areas such as SoHo, Midtown, Chinatown and the Upper East Side should avoid blocking portable fire extinguishers with displays, merchandise, boxes or other equipment.

Managing multiple extinguishers or commercial locations?

Property managers and businesses with several fire extinguishers can contact us to discuss the number of units, locations and service needs.
