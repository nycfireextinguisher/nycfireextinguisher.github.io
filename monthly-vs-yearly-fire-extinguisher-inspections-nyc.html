Monthly vs Yearly Fire Extinguisher Inspections in NYC

FDNY Compliance • Fire Safety • Commercial Buildings

Why Inspections Matter

Fire extinguishers should be inspected regularly to help ensure they are ready for use during an emergency and remain compliant with fire safety requirements.

Monthly vs Yearly Inspections

Monthly Inspection Yearly Inspection
Performed by building staff or responsible personnel. Performed by a certified fire extinguisher professional.
Visual inspection only. Detailed maintenance and testing.
No certification required. Professional inspection tag and documentation.
Basic record keeping. Comprehensive documentation and service records.

Monthly Inspection Checklist

  • Verify extinguisher is visible
  • Check extinguisher is accessible
  • Inspect pressure gauge
  • Look for physical damage
  • Confirm inspection tag is attached

Annual Inspection Benefits

  • Professional evaluation
  • Maintenance and testing
  • Updated inspection tag
  • Compliance documentation
  • Improved fire safety readiness
