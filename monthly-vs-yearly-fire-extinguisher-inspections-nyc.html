Monthly vs Annual Fire Extinguisher Inspection: What's the Difference?

They are two different types of fire extinguisher checks.

The monthly inspection is a routine quick check. The annual service is performed by a W-96 Certificate of Fitness holder working for an FDNY-approved portable fire extinguisher company.

FDNY guidance requires portable fire extinguishers to be checked monthly. The business owner is responsible for selecting a person to perform this monthly inspection, which FDNY refers to as a “quick check.”

At least annually, portable fire extinguishers must receive professional service from a W-96 Certificate of Fitness holder employed by an FDNY-approved company. After the annual service, the W-96 holder replaces the portable fire extinguisher tag and records the annual service information on the new tag.

That means a monthly inspection does not replace annual professional servicing, and annual servicing does not eliminate the need for monthly quick checks.

What Is Checked During the Monthly Quick Check?

FDNY identifies several basic conditions to review each month

✓

Fully Charged

✓

Designated Location

Check that the extinguisher does not show evidence of being actuated or tampered with.

✓

What Happens During Annual Service?

Annual service is handled differently from the monthly quick check

1

Qualified Service

Annual portable fire extinguisher service is performed by a W-96 Certificate of Fitness holder working for an FDNY-approved servicing company.

2

Equipment Service

The extinguisher receives professional service rather than only the routine visual quick check performed each month.

3

New PFE Tag

After annual service, the W-96 holder replaces the portable fire extinguisher tag and records the annual service information.

Why Do NYC Businesses Need Both?

A fire extinguisher can develop a problem between annual service visits. It could be moved, discharged, damaged, tampered with or otherwise develop a condition that affects its readiness.

The monthly quick check provides a recurring opportunity to identify obvious problems. Annual professional service provides the separate professional service required for portable fire extinguishers.

Restaurants

Restaurant equipment, storage boxes and supplies can sometimes interfere with access to portable fire extinguishers. Regular monthly checks help restaurant staff notice these issues.

Retail Stores & Offices

Merchandise displays, office furniture and stored materials can change throughout the year. Businesses should make sure their extinguishers remain in the designated location and ready for use.

Warehouses & Commercial Buildings

Larger facilities may contain multiple portable fire extinguishers. Consistent monthly records can help businesses and property managers keep track of their equipment.

Monthly vs Annual Inspection FAQ

Are monthly and annual fire extinguisher inspections the same?

No. The monthly inspection is a routine quick check. Annual service is professional servicing performed by a W-96 Certificate of Fitness holder working for an FDNY-approved portable fire extinguisher company.

Who can perform the monthly fire extinguisher inspection?

FDNY guidance states that the business owner is responsible for selecting a person to perform the monthly quick check.

Does the monthly inspection require a W-96 Certificate of Fitness?

No. FDNY guidance distinguishes the monthly quick check from annual professional servicing. The W-96 requirement applies to the annual portable fire extinguisher service.

Who performs the annual fire extinguisher service?

At least annually, portable fire extinguishers must be serviced by a W-96 Certificate of Fitness holder from an FDNY-approved portable fire extinguisher company.

Does annual service replace the monthly inspection?

No. Monthly quick checks and annual professional service are separate parts of portable fire extinguisher inspection and servicing.
