When Might a Fire Extinguisher Need Recharge?

The correct service depends on the extinguisher and its condition

1

After Discharge

If a fire extinguisher has been used, even briefly, it should be professionally evaluated before being returned to service.

2

Pressure Concern

If an extinguisher equipped with a pressure gauge shows an abnormal reading, professional evaluation may be appropriate.

3

Partial Discharge

An extinguisher that has been partially discharged should not simply be placed back in its original location without evaluation.

4

Service Identifies a Need

Inspection or maintenance may identify a condition where recharge or another type of service should be considered.

5

Unknown Condition

If the extinguisher's service history or current condition is unclear, professional evaluation can help determine the appropriate next step.

6

Commercial Equipment

Businesses with multiple extinguishers can have their units evaluated to identify which equipment may require additional service.
