Fire Extinguisher Service for Queens Businesses

Queens includes busy restaurant districts, retail corridors, office spaces, warehouses, industrial properties and other commercial businesses. Fire extinguisher service needs can vary depending on the property, equipment and business type.

Flushing has a large concentration of restaurants, food-service businesses, supermarkets and retail stores. Portable fire extinguishers should remain visible and accessible and should not be blocked by inventory, boxes, displays or other equipment.
