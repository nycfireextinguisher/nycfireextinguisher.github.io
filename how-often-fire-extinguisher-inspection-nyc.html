NYC Fire Extinguisher Inspection Requirements

Fire extinguishers in NYC commercial buildings, restaurants, offices, warehouses, and retail stores should be inspected regularly to help maintain compliance and fire safety.

Portable fire extinguishers should also receive monthly visual inspections to make sure they are accessible, properly charged, and undamaged.

Common Inspection Schedule

  • Monthly visual inspection
  • Annual professional inspection
  • Recharge after use
  • Periodic hydrostatic testing depending on extinguisher type
