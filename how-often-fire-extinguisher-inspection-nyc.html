How Often Do Fire Extinguishers Need to Be Inspected in NYC?

Short answer: every month, plus annual professional service.

Portable fire extinguishers in New York City are required to receive a monthly quick check. They must also receive professional servicing at least annually.

According to FDNY guidance, the monthly inspection is a quick visual check. The building or business owner designates a person to perform the monthly inspection, and the inspection record should include the date and the name or initials of the person who performed the check.

Annual servicing is more extensive. FDNY guidance states that portable fire extinguishers must be serviced at least annually by a W-96 Certificate of Fitness holder working for an FDNY-approved portable fire extinguisher company.

This distinction is important because a monthly inspection and annual servicing are not the same thing.

What Should Be Checked Every Month?

The monthly inspection is a quick visual check of the extinguisher

✓

Correct Location

Confirm that the extinguisher remains in its designated location.

✓

Accessible

Make sure the extinguisher is readily accessible and not blocked by boxes, furniture or equipment.

✓

Pressure / Charge

For extinguishers with a pressure gauge, check that the gauge indicates the proper operating range.

✓

Physical Condition

Look for obvious physical damage or conditions that could prevent proper operation.

✓

No Evidence of Use or Tampering

Check that the extinguisher does not appear to have been actuated or tampered with.

✓

Record the Inspection

Record the date and the name or initials of the person performing the monthly quick check.

Monthly inspection is not the same as annual servicing.

The monthly quick check is a routine visual inspection. Annual servicing is a more detailed professional service and must be performed by the appropriately qualified service provider under NYC requirements.

Why Regular Fire Extinguisher Inspection Matters

Regular inspection helps identify problems before an emergency occurs. A portable fire extinguisher that is blocked, damaged, discharged or not properly charged may not be ready when it is needed.

Restaurants

Restaurants often have busy kitchens, storage areas and customer spaces. Fire extinguishers should remain visible and accessible and should not be blocked by food supplies, boxes or restaurant equipment.

Offices & Retail Stores

Office furniture, merchandise displays and stored materials can sometimes interfere with access to extinguishers. Monthly checks help identify these issues.

Warehouses & Commercial Buildings

Larger commercial properties may have multiple portable fire extinguishers. Keeping an organized monthly inspection record can help property managers track equipment throughout the building.

Who can perform the monthly fire extinguisher inspection?
