NYC Fire Safety provides commercial fire extinguisher maintenance service for businesses throughout New York City. Fire extinguishers can require additional attention because of use, physical condition, pressure concerns or other service needs.

Fire Extinguisher Maintenance Service

Maintenance needs depend on the extinguisher and its condition

1

Equipment Condition

The extinguisher can be evaluated for visible condition and indications that additional service may be appropriate.

2

Pressure Concerns

An abnormal pressure indication may require professional evaluation to determine the appropriate next step.

3

Used Extinguishers

An extinguisher that has been discharged should be professionally evaluated before it is returned to service.

4

Hose & Nozzle Condition

Visible hose, nozzle and related components can be evaluated for damage, obstruction or other service concerns.

5

Safety Components

Pins, seals, handles and other visible components may be evaluated as part of determining the condition of the equipment.

6

Recharge Evaluation

When appropriate, the extinguisher can be evaluated to determine whether recharge or another type of service is needed.

When Might an Extinguisher Need Service?

Some common situations where professional evaluation may be appropriate

!

The Extinguisher Was Used

A discharged or partially discharged extinguisher should not simply be placed back on the wall.

!

Pressure Looks Abnormal

If the pressure indicator appears outside its expected range, professional evaluation may be needed.

!

Visible Damage

Damage to the cylinder, handle, hose, nozzle or other components should not be ignored.

!

Missing or Damaged Components

Missing pins, seals, hoses or other visible parts may indicate that service is required.

!

Inspection Identifies a Problem

An inspection may identify a condition that requires additional maintenance rather than routine inspection alone.

!

Service History Is Unclear

If the condition or previous service history of an extinguisher is uncertain, professional evaluation can help determine the next step.

Fire Extinguisher Maintenance FAQ

What is the difference between inspection and maintenance?

An inspection can help identify visible conditions and service concerns. Maintenance involves additional professional service when the extinguisher's condition or service needs require it.

Does a used fire extinguisher need maintenance?

A discharged extinguisher should be professionally evaluated before being returned to service. The appropriate service depends on the extinguisher and its condition.

Can a fire extinguisher with low pressure be recharged?

That depends on the extinguisher type and condition. The unit should be evaluated before deciding whether recharge or another service is appropriate.

Do you provide maintenance for restaurants?

Yes. Restaurants and food-service businesses are among the commercial customers we serve throughout NYC.

Do you provide fire extinguisher maintenance throughout NYC?

Yes. We provide commercial fire extinguisher service for customers throughout Manhattan, Queens, Brooklyn, the Bronx and Staten Island.
