Commercial Fire Extinguisher Service in New York City

Fire extinguishers are an important part of fire safety for restaurants, stores, offices, warehouses and other commercial properties throughout New York City. Keeping extinguishers accessible and in appropriate condition helps businesses stay prepared when an emergency occurs.

NYC Fire Safety provides fire extinguisher inspection, maintenance and recharge services for commercial customers throughout Manhattan, Queens, Brooklyn, the Bronx and Staten Island.

Need service for multiple fire extinguishers?

We work with restaurants, property managers, commercial buildings and businesses that need service for multiple extinguishers at one location or across multiple locations.

Restaurant Fire Extinguisher Service

Restaurants and food-service businesses operate around cooking equipment, electrical equipment and busy customer areas. Portable fire extinguishers should remain accessible and in appropriate condition. We provide commercial extinguisher service for restaurants, takeout businesses, cafés and other food-service locations.

Office & Commercial Building Service

Commercial buildings may have multiple extinguishers throughout offices, hallways, common areas and other spaces. We help businesses and property managers organize inspection and service for multiple units.

Stores & Warehouses

Inventory, shelving and changing floor layouts can sometimes block or interfere with access to fire extinguishers. Regular inspection helps identify visible problems before they are overlooked.
