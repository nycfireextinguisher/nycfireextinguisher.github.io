What We Look for During a Fire Extinguisher Inspection

Common visible conditions that may require attention

1

Accessibility

The extinguisher should be visible and should not be blocked by boxes, furniture, inventory or equipment.

2

Physical Condition

The extinguisher is visually reviewed for obvious damage or other conditions that may require attention.

3

Pressure Indicator

For extinguishers equipped with a pressure gauge, the visible gauge condition and reading can be reviewed.

4

Hose & Nozzle

Visible hose and nozzle components can be checked for obvious damage, obstruction or missing parts.

5

Safety Pin & Seal

Visible safety components can be checked for condition and signs that the extinguisher may have been used or disturbed.

6

Service Information

Existing inspection or service information can be reviewed to help determine whether additional professional service is needed.

Fire Extinguisher Inspection FAQ

Do you provide fire extinguisher inspection throughout NYC?

Yes. We provide commercial fire extinguisher service for customers throughout Manhattan, Queens, Brooklyn, the Bronx and Staten Island.

Do you inspect fire extinguishers for restaurants?

Yes. Restaurants and food-service businesses are among the commercial customers we serve.

What happens if an extinguisher needs maintenance?

If an extinguisher requires additional service, the appropriate maintenance can be determined based on the extinguisher type and its condition.

Can a used fire extinguisher simply be put back on the wall?

A discharged extinguisher should be professionally evaluated before being returned to service. Depending on the unit and its condition, additional maintenance or recharge may be appropriate.

Can you inspect multiple extinguishers at a commercial property?

Yes. Businesses with multiple fire extinguishers can contact us with their location and approximate number of units to discuss service.
