Fire Extinguisher Service for Bronx Businesses

The Bronx includes a wide range of commercial properties, from restaurants and neighborhood stores to warehouses, offices, supermarkets and larger commercial buildings. Fire extinguisher needs can vary depending on the type of business and the hazards present at the property.

Restaurants and Commercial Kitchens

Restaurants should keep portable fire extinguishers visible and accessible and should pay attention to their condition and service information. Commercial kitchens may also use separate fire suppression equipment designed for cooking-related hazards.

Warehouses and Industrial Businesses

Warehouse and industrial spaces may contain larger floor areas, inventory, electrical equipment and different fire hazards. Fire extinguishers should remain easy to locate and should not be blocked by stored merchandise or equipment.

Retail Stores and Offices

Retail stores and offices should also keep portable fire extinguishers accessible and should not allow furniture, displays or inventory to interfere with access to fire protection equipment.
