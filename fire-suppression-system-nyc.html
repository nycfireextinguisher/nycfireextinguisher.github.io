What Is a Commercial Fire Suppression System?

Commercial kitchens present a different fire risk than ordinary offices or retail spaces. Cooking appliances, hot surfaces, oils and grease can create conditions where a fire can spread quickly.

A commercial kitchen fire suppression system is designed to provide automatic fire protection around cooking equipment and the kitchen hood area when a fire occurs.

Restaurants and other food-service businesses should pay attention to the condition of their fire suppression equipment and arrange appropriate professional service when inspection or maintenance is needed.

Common System Components

  • Suppression agent cylinder
  • Detection components
  • Discharge piping and nozzles
  • Manual pull station
  • Kitchen hood protection
  • Cooking appliance protection
  • System service and inspection components

How a Kitchen Fire Suppression System Works

A basic overview of commercial kitchen fire protection

1

Fire Is Detected

The system is designed to respond when fire conditions are detected around protected cooking equipment.

2

System Activates

The suppression system activates and begins the fire-control process.

3

Agent Discharges

Suppression agent is directed toward protected cooking appliances and hazard areas.

4

Fire Is Controlled

The system is intended to help suppress the cooking fire and reduce the risk of it spreading.

When Should You Call for Service?

Do not ignore visible problems with commercial fire protection equipment

System Has Been Activated

If a suppression system has discharged or activated, professional service should be arranged before relying on the system again.

Visible Damage or Missing Components

Damaged nozzles, piping, pull stations or other visible components should not be ignored.

Restaurant Equipment Has Changed

Changes to the cooking line or kitchen equipment can affect the relationship between appliances and the existing suppression system. The system should be professionally evaluated when significant equipment changes are made.

Service or Inspection Is Due

If your restaurant's fire suppression system is due for service or you are unsure of its current status, schedule a professional evaluation.

Fire Suppression System FAQ

Is a fire suppression system the same as a fire extinguisher?

No. A portable fire extinguisher is manually operated. A commercial kitchen fire suppression system is a separate system designed to protect specific cooking equipment and kitchen hazard areas.

Do restaurants use both fire extinguishers and fire suppression systems?

Commercial kitchens may have several different types of fire protection equipment. Portable fire extinguishers and kitchen suppression systems perform different roles.

What happens after a kitchen suppression system activates?

A discharged or activated system should be professionally evaluated and serviced before being returned to normal use.
