Why Fire Extinguisher Inspection Matters for NYC Restaurants

Restaurants operate around cooking equipment, electrical equipment, heat, grease and busy customer areas. Portable fire extinguishers are an important part of a restaurant's overall fire protection equipment.

Simply having an extinguisher hanging on the wall is not enough. Restaurant owners and managers should pay attention to whether extinguishers remain visible, accessible and in appropriate condition.

Inspection tags, extinguisher pressure, physical condition and placement can all become important when maintaining commercial fire safety.

A fire extinguisher should be ready before you actually need it.

Do not wait until an extinguisher has been discharged, damaged or forgotten behind restaurant supplies before checking its condition.

Common Restaurant Fire Extinguisher Problems

Small problems are easy to overlook in a busy restaurant

!

Old or Missing Service Tag

Restaurant staff may not notice that an extinguisher's service information needs attention.

!

Pressure Gauge Concern

A pressure gauge showing an abnormal reading should not simply be ignored.

!

Blocked Extinguisher

Boxes, chairs, food supplies or restaurant equipment can make an extinguisher difficult to reach.

!

Physical Damage

Damage to the cylinder, hose, handle, pin or other visible components may require professional attention.

!

Extinguisher Has Been Used

An extinguisher that has been discharged should be professionally evaluated before being returned to service.

!

Improper Location

An extinguisher should not be moved to a location where employees cannot easily see or access it.

Restaurant Fire Extinguisher Checklist

Simple things restaurant owners and managers can watch for

✓
Keep extinguishers visible Do not hide extinguishers behind boxes, furniture, equipment or restaurant supplies.
✓
Keep access clear Employees should be able to reach the extinguisher without moving obstacles first.
✓
Look at the extinguisher's condition Watch for obvious physical damage or missing components.
✓
Pay attention to the pressure gauge An abnormal gauge reading is a reason to arrange professional evaluation.
✓
Review service information Do not wait until service information has been forgotten before arranging the next inspection.
✓
Have used equipment evaluated If an extinguisher has been discharged, arrange professional service before relying on it again.

Restaurant Fire Extinguisher FAQ

Why shouldn't a restaurant block a fire extinguisher?

In an emergency, employees need to be able to locate and reach appropriate fire safety equipment quickly. Boxes, furniture and restaurant supplies should not interfere with access.

What should I do if the extinguisher pressure looks wrong?

Arrange professional evaluation rather than assuming the extinguisher is ready for use.

What if a restaurant fire extinguisher has already been used?

A discharged extinguisher should be professionally evaluated and, when appropriate for the unit, serviced or recharged before being returned to service.

Does a commercial kitchen also need a fire suppression system?

Portable fire extinguishers and commercial kitchen suppression systems serve different fire-protection functions. The requirements for a particular restaurant depend on its equipment, layout and applicable rules.
