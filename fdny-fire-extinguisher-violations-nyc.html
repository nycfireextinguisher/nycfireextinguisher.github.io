Common Fire Extinguisher Issues NYC Businesses Should Watch For

Fire extinguishers are an important part of commercial fire safety, but they can easily be overlooked during the normal operation of a busy restaurant, store, office or warehouse.

An extinguisher may still be hanging in its usual location while having an outdated service tag, an abnormal pressure reading, physical damage or restricted access.

These types of problems can create fire-safety and compliance concerns. Regular visual checks and appropriate professional inspection and maintenance can help businesses identify issues before they are forgotten.

Do not wait until an inspection or emergency to notice a problem.

Fire extinguishers should remain visible, accessible and in appropriate condition so they are ready when needed.

Common Fire Extinguisher Problems

Examples of issues commercial businesses should not ignore

1

Outdated or Missing Service Tag

Service information should not be ignored or allowed to become difficult to verify.

2

Missing Fire Extinguisher

A required extinguisher that has been removed, lost or relocated may leave an area without the intended portable fire protection.

3

Blocked or Inaccessible Extinguisher

Boxes, furniture, inventory or equipment can make an extinguisher difficult to reach during an emergency.

4

Abnormal Pressure Reading

A pressure gauge outside its expected operating range is a reason to arrange professional evaluation.

5

Damaged Equipment

Visible damage to the cylinder, hose, handle, safety pin or other components should not be ignored.

6

Mounting or Placement Problems

Extinguishers should remain appropriately located and should not be moved to inconvenient or hidden areas.

7

Extinguisher Selection Concerns

Different hazards can require different types of portable fire extinguishers. Businesses should use equipment appropriate for their specific environment.

8

Inspection or Service Overlooked

Busy businesses can lose track of extinguisher inspection and service needs if records are not reviewed regularly.

How Businesses Can Reduce Fire Safety Problems

Simple habits can help businesses keep fire extinguishers organized

✓
Keep extinguishers visible and accessible Avoid placing boxes, merchandise, furniture or equipment in front of fire extinguishers.
✓
Review extinguisher condition Watch for visible damage, missing components or other obvious changes in condition.
✓
Pay attention to pressure An abnormal pressure reading should be evaluated rather than ignored.
✓
Keep track of inspection and service information Businesses should maintain awareness of when extinguishers were inspected or serviced.
✓
Arrange service after extinguisher use A discharged extinguisher should be professionally evaluated before being placed back into service.
✓
Use appropriate fire protection equipment Restaurants, commercial kitchens, offices and other businesses may have different fire hazards and equipment needs.

NYC Fire Extinguisher FAQ

Can a blocked fire extinguisher be a problem?

Yes. Fire extinguishers should remain accessible so appropriate equipment can be reached quickly during an emergency.

What should I do if an extinguisher has low or abnormal pressure?

Arrange professional evaluation rather than assuming the extinguisher remains ready for use.

What if a fire extinguisher has already been discharged?

A discharged extinguisher should be professionally evaluated. Depending on the extinguisher and its condition, appropriate service may include recharge or other maintenance.

Are all fire extinguishers appropriate for every type of business?

No. Different fire hazards can require different types of extinguishing equipment. The appropriate equipment depends on the hazards present in the area.
