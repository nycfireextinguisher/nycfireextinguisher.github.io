Common FDNY Fire Extinguisher Issues

Restaurants, offices, stores, warehouses, and commercial buildings in NYC can receive FDNY violations for fire safety problems and missing maintenance items.

Regular inspection and maintenance can help businesses stay organized and improve fire safety readiness.

Common Fire Extinguisher Violations

  • Expired inspection tag
  • Missing fire extinguisher
  • Blocked or inaccessible extinguisher
  • Low pressure gauge reading
  • Damaged or leaking extinguisher
  • Improper extinguisher mounting
  • Wrong extinguisher type for the area
  • Missing annual inspection

How Businesses Can Help Avoid Violations
